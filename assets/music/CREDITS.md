# Music

Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/pleasant-creek (https://opengameart.org/node/21503)

Changes: levelled and re-encoded from the pack's WAV to OGG Vorbis by `checks/build_music.py`.

`canoodling.ogg` (the CANOODLING game) is **"Beaver's Sweet Canoe"** and `dam_it.ogg` (DAM IT!) is **"Woodland Quest"**,
both supplied by the project team. Changes: decoded from M4A, levelled, the end folded over the start so each loops
without a seam, and encoded to OGG Vorbis by `checks/build_music.py --out NAME SONG`.
